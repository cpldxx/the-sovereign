What it can do

Each of these runs in the lab today.

  • Scholarly search. OpenAlex, arXiv and PubMed, with open-access full text.
  • Its own search engine. A local SearXNG, or Tavily and Brave with a free key.
  • Pages that need a browser. JavaScript-rendered sites, and PDFs up to 60 pages.
  • Graph search by meaning. The closest entities and facts, the strongest facts around them, and their sources.
  • Synthesis pages. Per core question: the consensus, the debates, the open questions, the key sources.
  • A briefing every cycle. What it learned, what changed, what needs your attention. It can read it aloud.
  • Voice. Talk to the Head and hear it answer; speech recognition runs on your machine.
  • Many topics, many people. A database per topic; share one as viewer, editor or owner.
  • A nightly security check. No answer without sign-in, no reach across topics, no page past robots.txt.
  • It recovers on its own. After a crash it picks up the pages it had read and waits while the machine is short of memory.

It doesn't act in the world: no messages, orders or bookings. It finds out what is true and explains it.
